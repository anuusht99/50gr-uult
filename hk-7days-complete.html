Safety & Important Notes

Safety: Hong Kong is one of Asia's safest cities. Excellent public transport, clear signage in English. Solo travelers very safe. 24/7 tourism support available.
Weather (November): Perfect season! 15-25°C, low humidity, occasional light rain. Bring light jacket & umbrella.
Currency: Hong Kong Dollar (HKD). 1 HKD ≈ 3,600-3,700 MNT. Exchange at airport or use ATMs for best rates.
Mobile: Buy local SIM card at airport. 3HK, SmarTone, or CSL offer good tourist packages.
